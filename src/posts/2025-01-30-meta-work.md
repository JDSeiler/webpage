# Meta-work
Some people, like myself, find themselves prone to an insidious productivity
killer. It's a subculture that presents itself as being *all about*
productivity, when in reality it is a siren song that draws us away from real
work. I call this phenomenon "meta-work" and I want to talk about what I
think it is, how it can harm us, and what we can do about it.

# What is Meta-work?
Meta-work is a preoccupation with the tools and processes used to support your
actual work. It is **not** anything to do with the tools or processes
themselves, but rather how an individual is relating to those things.

Common examples of things people use to meta-work are:
- Todo list management apps:
  - Omnifocus
  - Things
  - Todoist
- Project management software
  - Asana
  - Jira
- Time tracking software
  - Toggl
  - Timery
  - Timewarrior
- Knowledge and Note managers:
  - Rome
  - Notion
  - Obsidian
  - Org-mode
- "Philosophies":
  - Eat the Frog
  - Getting Things Done
  - Kanban
  - Lean
  - Scrum
  - OKRs (Objectives and Key Results)
  - Pomodoro
- "Frameworks":
  - Bullet Journal Method
  - Zettelkasten

To reiterate, nothing is wrong with these tools or ideas. In many cases they are highly
valuable and *should* be used. But, like almost everything, they can be used poorly.

The meta-worker has lost sight of the difference between accomplishming a task
and how the process made them feel. They are less interested in how a tool will
help them with what they're *actually* trying to do as opposed to what a tool
*might* enable them to do.

To the meta-worker, the grass is always greener. They are constantly chasing the
"magic" addition to their "personal productivity system" that will make all
their dreams come true. The irony is that it was never about the dreams. It was
never about accomplishing their goals. Instead, it is entirely about how the process
of existing in the world makes them feel. It is a dysfunctional way to cope with
anxiety. Since most of life cannot be controlled, they will rigidly control other
parts of their life with software and routines and methods.
